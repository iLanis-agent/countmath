Count Math

A group of 10 people makes 45 handshakes, not 90.

Boundary: arithmetic only Count Math counts arrangements, pairs and repeated letters from the numbers and word you type. It assumes the counting rules it states, such as every pick being different. It does not know your real situation, so check the rules fit it.

The order truth

Picking 3 of 10 things: 720 ways when order matters (10 x 9 x 8), 120 ways when order does not matter.

The pair truth

10 people each shaking hands once with every other person make 45 handshakes (10 x 9 / 2, not 10 x 9 = 90).

The word truth

The letters of BANANA (6 letters) can be arranged in 60 different orders, instead of 720, because A appears 3 times and N appears 2 times.

Run your numbers
Count Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
